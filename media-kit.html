01

What if you woke up,
and everyone was gone?

Kilmaru films the world's most iconic cities completely empty. Times Square with no crowds. Shibuya Crossing in silence. Airports, hotels, stadiums, cruise ships: abandoned, as if humanity had vanished overnight.

First person, no face, no dialogue. Just atmospheric sound and the feeling of being the last person alive, somewhere between social media and short film. Since 2021 it has become its own genre, solitude exploration, and it travels across every culture because it has no language at all.

04

Brand integrations
filmed as cinema.

In an empty world, nothing competes for attention. No crowds, no noise, no other brands in frame. A car, a hotel, a destination, a product: whatever enters the shot becomes the only thing left on Earth, and there is nowhere else to look.

▶ 18.3M views

The last drive

A solitary luxury car glides through the empty streets of a sleeping metropolis. No traffic, no pedestrians, no other headlights. Only the engine, echoing between skyscrapers. Kilmaru behind the wheel, silent.

Fit: automotive · luxury cars · urban transportation

▶ 9.9M views

Sound of silence

Footsteps through an empty terminal. A moving walkway with no one on it. Kilmaru lifts a pair of headphones. Music breaks the vacuum. The contrast between absolute silence and premium sound. Felt, not explained.

Fit: audio · headphones · airlines · airport retail

▶ 15.3M views

Empty runway

A grand shopping arcade, every store open, not a single person inside. Every step frames the sneakers, the jacket, the silhouette. No crowd to compete with. The clothing holds the shot on its own.

Fit: fashion · sneakers · luxury retail

▶ 17.9M views

The last guest

A hotel with every light still on and not a soul behind the desk. Corridors, a lit pool, a rooftop with a view no one else is watching. The camera moves slowly. The brand isn't in the frame. It is the frame.

Fit: hospitality · hotels · tourism boards · cruise lines
