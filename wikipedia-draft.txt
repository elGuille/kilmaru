{{Short description|Anonymous video creator known for "empty world" videos}}

'''Kilmaru''' is the pseudonym of an anonymous video creator who posts first-person videos in which well-known cities and public places appear to contain no other people. The account began posting on [[TikTok]] in 2021 under the handle ''kilianandmaruta''. The videos have no dialogue and do not show the person filming.<ref name="wired"/><ref name="newsweek"/>

== Videos ==
The first videos, posted in 2021, showed empty streets, supermarkets and a [[McDonald's]] restaurant.<ref name="wired"/> Later clips showed city centres including Rome, New York, London and Barcelona without any people in them, as well as the [[Vatican Museums]] and the [[Colosseum]].<ref name="wired"/> Airports, hotels, stadiums and cruise ships have also appeared.<ref name="geopop"/>

In the narrative presented on the account, the creator describes himself as a former programmer of artificial intelligence tools who was transported to an uninhabited parallel world after hacking a quantum computer.<ref name="geopop"/> In June 2021 the account posted a five-part series in which the narrator says he woke up on a deserted cruise ship. ''[[Newsweek]]'' described it as a continuation of the "empty world" series begun that March.<ref name="newsweek"/>

By February 2024 the account had about four million followers on TikTok.<ref name="wired"/><ref name="geopop"/>

== Method ==
The fact-checking site ''[[Snopes]]'' examined the videos and concluded that they are recorded normally and that other people are removed afterwards in [[post-production]].<ref name="snopes"/> ''Geopop'' reached the same conclusion, describing the use of object-removal software comparable to Google's Magic Eraser, and reported that the videos are filmed early in the morning to reduce the amount of editing needed.<ref name="geopop"/> The visual effects channel [[Corridor Crew]] analysed the videos in a 2022 episode of its ''VFX Artists React'' series.<ref name="corridor"/>

== Coverage ==
The videos have been reported on in several countries. In Italy they were covered by ''[[Corriere della Sera]]'',<ref name="corriere"/> ''[[Wired (magazine)|Wired Italia]]'',<ref name="wired"/> ''[[Fanpage.it]]''<ref name="fanpage"/> and ''Geopop''.<ref name="geopop"/> In the United States they were covered by ''Newsweek'',<ref name="newsweek"/> Yahoo's ''In The Know''<ref name="yahoo"/> and ''[[The Daily Dot]]''.<ref name="dailydot"/> In France they were mentioned by ''[[20 Minutes (France)|20 Minutes]]'' in an article about accounts that present themselves as travellers from other realities.<ref name="20min"/>

== Award ==
At the 2021 Monaco Streaming Film Festival the award for Best Creative Content went to the account, credited as "Kilian and Maruta".<ref name="monaco"/>
