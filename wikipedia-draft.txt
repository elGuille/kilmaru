{{Short description|Anonymous video creator known for "empty world" videos}}
[[File:Kilmaru.png|thumb|Kilmaru]]

'''Kilmaru''' is the pseudonym used by an anonymous video creator whose first-person videos depict famous cities and public locations as though no other people are present.<ref name="corriere"/> The account started publishing videos on [[TikTok]] in 2021.<ref name="wired"/><ref name="newsweek"/> The videos contain no dialogue, and the individual behind the camera never appears on screen.<ref name="corriere"/>

== Videos ==
The earliest uploads featured deserted streets, supermarkets and a [[McDonald's]] restaurant.<ref name="wired"/> Later videos expanded to city centres including New York, London, Rome, Barcelona as well as the [[Vatican Museums]] and the [[Colosseum]], each shown without any visible people.<ref name="wired"/> Other videos have shown Berlin, [[Tokyo]], Florence and Pisa in broad daylight, with no pedestrians, no museum visitors, and no customers or employees visible inside shops.<ref name="corriere"/> The locations are places that are usually busy, such as [[Piccadilly Circus]] and [[Times Square]]. A repeated line appears throughout the videos: "I woke up here and I am alone".<ref name="corriere"/>

According to the narrative presented on the account, the narrator previously worked as a programmer of artificial intelligence systems and arrived in an uninhabited parallel world after hacking a quantum computer.<ref name="geopop"/> In June 2021, a five-part series showed the narrator waking up aboard an abandoned cruise ship. ''[[Newsweek]]'' described the series as a continuation of the "empty world" videos, which had begun in March of that year.<ref name="newsweek"/>

Media organisations in the United State France and Italy have covered the videos.<ref name="newsweek"/><ref name="yahoo"/><ref name="corriere"/><ref name="wired"/><ref name="fanpage"/><ref name="20min"/>

== Production ==
The fact-checking website ''[[Snopes]]'' examined the cruise ship videos and concluded that they had been filmed under normal conditions before the other people were removed during [[post-production]].<ref name="snopes"/> ''Geopop'' reported the same process, describing the use of object-removal software similar to Google's Magic Eraser together with filming early in the morning, when fewer people are present and need to be removed.<ref name="geopop"/> The visual effects channel [[Corridor Crew]] dedicated an episode of its ''VFX Artists React'' series to the videos.<ref name="corridor"/>

== Recognition ==
During the 2021 Monaco Streaming Film Festival, the account received the Best Creative Content award. The winners were credited as the duo "Kilian and Maruta".<ref name="monaco"/>
